## Spatial reading

- Floor II / archive: a broken dark seam appears in the retained record wall.
- Floor III / possibility: two structural plates repeat the relation but occupy
  different heights and depths.
- Floor IV / distinction: two long beams and one brace form an apparent `≠`
  only near the registered camera view (`yaw = 3.80 rad`, `pitch = 0.42 rad`).

The floor-IV brace is deliberately not planar. Its centreline changes depth
along its length, while world-space height is compensated so that the registered
orthographic projection is straight. Rotating the camera reveals the hidden
depth and the brace bends apart visually. The two beams are oriented along the
registered screen-horizontal basis, so they also tilt outside the solution view.

This is the Monument-Valley-like mechanism: the completed sign exists in a
projection, not as a flat symbol attached to a facade.
