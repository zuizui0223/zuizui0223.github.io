## Azores — post-activation speed audit

Six-project exact replay: n=418, 13 project-year strata. Durif-stage speed ratio **0.9831 [0.8524, 1.1339]**. Project heterogeneity: **Q=2.469, df=5, p=.781**; leave-one-project-out pooled ratio **.953–1.029**. The broad whole-migration near-null is therefore not explained by strong opposing project effects. Current interpretation: activation and progression are distinct, and post-activation state effects may be scale/context dependent.
