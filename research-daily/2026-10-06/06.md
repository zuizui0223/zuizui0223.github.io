## BITA — effective reach changes the participation interpretation

[Hummingbird sensitivity](https://github.com/zuizui0223/bita/blob/main/empirical/floral_defence_selectivity/results/aubert2026_hummingbird_effective_reach_sensitivity.json) is post-open, not independent confirmation. With a 1.8× effective-reach correction, legitimate-access RR is **0.154 [0.087,0.271]**, bypass/nectar-robbing RR **0.816 [0.358,1.860]**, and the bypass/legitimate RR ratio **5.311 [1.923,14.672]**. Thus severe mismatch mainly filters the legitimate route while bypass use is comparatively retained; absolute bypass counts need not rise. The continuous mismatch-to-routing association remains invariant to constant reach scaling.

## izu-core — phenotypic memory does not diagnose evolutionary arrest

draft [PR #402](https://github.com/zuizui0223/izu-core/pull/402) uses **4,096** finite trajectories: 200 updates of different visitor histories followed by 800 updates of identical current visitor exposure. At update 1000, delayed/costly populations retain investment differences of **−.1404** without mutation and **−.1301** with mutation; assurance differences are **+.0341** and **+.0558**. Zero-mutation populations retain one investment allele and late change=0, whereas mutation=.01 populations retain about **6.4–6.6 alleles** and continue changing. Same endpoint memory can therefore represent genetic arrest or ongoing evolution. This is a synthetic possibility, not a field validation.
